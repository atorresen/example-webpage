assignments

counter-composition

Choose a "rule" or "principle" of design. Using any materials, create a composition that demonstrates "exactly the opposite" of your chosen rule. (This is an in-class assignment; some basic collage materials will be provided, but you are encouraged to bring any other materials you would like.)

algorithm

Design instructions for an algorithm that creates a work of art, using the algorithmically-selected materials you chose from Second Life. Execute the algorithm, then find at least two other people to execute the algorithm. Document your instructions and the results.

generative series

Write p5.js code that uses random() to generate a new random image every time the code is run. Curate a series of 3-5 images that result from your code. Submit your selected images and the code that created them (as a text file or as a link to your p5.js sketch).

midterm project

Students design their own midterm project to meet the following criteria:

  • combine at least 2 different mediums
  • reflect on class topics (see mind map for inspiration)
  • take ownership
  • take risks
soundscape

Go on a walk around Providence or to a specific place. Record sounds around you. Use a digial audio workstation (DAW) to combine/edit the sounds into a "soundscape" of the place where you recorded them. Export your soundscape as an audio file (.mp3, .mp4, or .wav) and give the work a title.
