      main ideas/questions: what is "rule-based art"? how is a design process related to design outcomes? how much control does an artist have over the outcome of their work? /// what does it mean to define and create within constraints? how can we balance structure and uncertainty? /// how does it feel to include other people in your design processes? how do different people interpret rules? how can we write rules to be open to interpretation?

      “The artist does not create the image directly but instead formulates the rules — the algorithm — that subsequently produces the artwork. ... While the artist specifies the input parameters [and process], they relinquish full control over the final outcome.”

      Arina Ospanova, 2025 Algorithmic Art: The Formation of the Genre and Its Perception in Contemporary Society

      due next class: define an algorithm using the algorithmically-chosen materials you selected during class; have at least two other people complete your algorithm; document your algorithm's rules and the results
